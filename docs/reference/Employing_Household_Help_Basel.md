## Purpose of this document
This guide helps private individuals in Basel-Stadt employ a household cleaner legally, fairly, and with as little administrative effort as possible[cite: 1]. Employing someone in your home makes you an employer under Swiss law; but for a few hours a week, the process is genuinely simple once it is set up[cite: 1]. This document, together with the companion spreadsheet, gives you everything you need: how to register, how to pay correctly, and what to do if something out of the ordinary comes up[cite: 1].

It is organised in two parts[cite: 1]. Part A: The Normal Case covers setting up and running a smooth, ongoing arrangement[cite: 1]. Part B: Exceptions is a reference for when things are not routine: illness, accidents, breakages, disputes, or ending the employment[cite: 1]. You only need Part B when something specific happens[cite: 1].

> **Note:** This guide is compiled from publicly available information (primarily the Ausgleichskasse Basel-Stadt, SECO, and the Swiss Code of Obligations) and reflects rates and procedures as of 2026, with a legal-source review completed in July 2026[cite: 1]. It is provided for general information only and is not legal, tax, or financial advice[cite: 1]. Rules change and individual situations differ; for a specific question, check the official sources linked throughout or consult the AWA or a qualified adviser[cite: 1].

# PART A: The Normal Case
## Setting up and running a smooth, ongoing arrangement

### BACKGROUND: How it all works
*German: vereinfachtes Abrechnungsverfahren (VAV): simplified settlement procedure*[cite: 3]

Switzerland created the simplified procedure specifically so that private households can employ domestic help without the heavy payroll administration a business faces[cite: 3]. It exists under the Federal Act against Illegal Employment (BGSA), which aims to bring casual domestic work into the formal system so that workers receive proper social insurance, accident cover, and pension contributions[cite: 3].

You register once with the cantonal compensation office (the Ausgleichskasse Basel-Stadt)[cite: 3]. From then on, all social insurance contributions: old-age pension (AHV), disability (IV), income replacement (EO), unemployment (ALV), family allowances (FAK), accident insurance, and withholding tax, are reported and settled once per year through that single office[cite: 3]. There is no monthly filing and no payroll software required[cite: 3].

The simplified procedure is available only if each employee earns no more than CHF 22,680 gross per year and the employer's total annual payroll does not exceed CHF 60,480[cite: 3]. All contributory wages must be included in the procedure[cite: 3]. A small household employment arrangement will usually remain below these limits[cite: 3].

> **Note:** Under the simplified procedure, deduct 6.4% employee AHV/IV/EO and ALV contributions and the flat 5% tax from each wage payment[cite: 3]. If the employee works at least 8 hours a week for this employer, also deduct the applicable NBU premium[cite: 3]. The employer reports the annual gross wage and settles the accumulated contributions and tax with the Ausgleichskasse once per year[cite: 3]. The employee receives a tax certificate from the Ausgleichskasse; the employer does not issue a separate Lohnausweis for wages settled through this procedure[cite: 3].

### SETUP: DO ONCE
#### Register with the Ausgleichskasse Basel-Stadt
*German: Ausgleichskasse: compensation fund/social insurance office*[cite: 4]

Registration is done through an online form on the AKBS website; there is nothing to print or post[cite: 4]. It takes about 15 minutes once you have the details below to hand[cite: 4].

First, gather these (the form asks for each):
*   Your name, date of birth, and home address[cite: 4].
*   Your employee's name, date of birth, and AHV number (756.XXXX.XXXX.XX)[cite: 4].
*   Your employee's home address (the simplified procedure handles their tax, so this is required)[cite: 4].
*   Whether your employee also works abroad (yes/no)[cite: 4].
*   The date wages start and an estimated annual gross total[cite: 4].

Then complete the form:
1.  **Open the Haushaltshilfen page:** Go to ak-bs.ch/ubersicht/beitrage-an-die-ahv-iv-eo/haushaltshilfen/ and scroll to the registration section[cite: 4].
2.  **Choose the simplified procedure:** Select "Abrechnung mit Steuerabzug, vereinfachtes Verfahren" (not "Standard-Abrechnung")[cite: 4].
3.  **Fill in employer, employee, and wage fields:** Enter all the details you gathered[cite: 4]. For "weitere Erwerbstätigkeit im Ausland" answer Yes only if your employee also works outside Switzerland[cite: 4].
4.  **Set the accident-insurance option (VAvplus):** At "Haben Sie eine Unfallversicherung abgeschlossen?" answer Nein[cite: 4]. Then at "Möchten Sie die Unfallversicherungsprämie direkt mit der Ausgleichskasse abrechnen?" answer Ja[cite: 4]. This bundles accident cover through the AKBS (via SOLIDA) so you need no separate insurer[cite: 4].
5.  **Choose electronic correspondence (optional):** You can opt to receive future correspondence via the online portal "connect" rather than by post[cite: 4].
6.  **Submit:** The AKBS confirms your registration by post and sends your member number (Mitgliedernummer) and, if chosen, the details needed to use the connect portal[cite: 4].

**Who can use the simplified procedure:**
*   Gross wage per employee does not exceed CHF 22,680 in 2026[cite: 4].
*   Total annual payroll does not exceed CHF 60,480 in 2026[cite: 4].
*   All contributory wages are settled through the simplified procedure[cite: 4].
*   No cross-border worker resident in France; ask AKBS about any other cross-border situation[cite: 4].
*   The employee is not the employer's spouse or child[cite: 4].

#### Sign an employment contract
*German: Arbeitsvertrag: employment contract*[cite: 5]

Use a written contract[cite: 5]. The official Basel-Stadt AWA hourly contract is the best starting point because it is designed for household staff in this canton and refers directly to the cantonal NAV[cite: 5].

**Download the current official template:**
Go to bs.ch/wsu/awa/arbeitsbeziehungen/arbeitsverhaeltnisse/normalarbeitsvertraege-nav and download NAV Vertragsmuster Hauspersonal Stundenlohn[cite: 5].

**For the regular paid-leave model used in this guide:**
*   State the agreed weekly hours and normal workday or workdays[cite: 5].
*   State the gross hourly wage (The companion workbook is preconfigured to CHF 28.50 gross per paid hour)[cite: 5].
*   State four weeks of holiday unless a five-week entitlement applies because of age[cite: 5].
*   Do not agree to a running monthly payout of holiday compensation for this regular weekly arrangement[cite: 5].
*   Add a written clarification such as: *Ferien werden effektiv bezogen. Der Ferienlohn wird beim Ferienbezug auf Basis des vereinbarten Bruttostundenlohns ausgerichtet. Keine laufende Auszahlung der Ferienentschädigung.*[cite: 5].
*   List the agreed tasks or attach a short task list[cite: 5].

The contract should also state names/addresses, start date, probation period, employee function, notice periods, and sickness reporting procedures[cite: 5].

#### Agree the scope and your working-style preferences
1.  **Define the tasks:** Write a simple list of what is included (e.g. kitchen, bathrooms, floors, dusting) and what is not[cite: 6].
2.  **Be clear about your working style:** Specify if you are happy with latitude (reorganization) or if you prefer precision (items left exactly where found)[cite: 6].
3.  **Put it somewhere both of you can refer to:** Attach the task list to the contract, or keep it as a shared note[cite: 6].

#### Prepare the payslip spreadsheet
Open Payslip_2026.xlsx and complete the blue cells on the Rates sheet[cite: 7]. Enter employer details, employee details, gross hourly wage, employment dates, regular weekly hours, and holiday entitlement[cite: 7]. The workbook determines whether NBU applies and pro-rates holiday entitlements[cite: 7].

### RUNNING IT
#### Calculate and pay monthly wages
1.  **Record the month:** Keep the dates and hours for each visit, plus any paid holiday, public-holiday or sick-leave hours[cite: 7].
2.  **Open the correct month tab:** Each month is already formatted as a payslip[cite: 7].
3.  **Enter paid hours:** B8: hours worked; B9: paid holiday hours; B10: paid public-holiday hours; B11: paid sick-leave hours[cite: 7].
4.  **Enter holiday work correctly:** If work was performed on a statutory holiday, enter hours in B8 and repeat in B12 for the 50% premium[cite: 7].
5.  **Enter any extras:** B13: cash bonus; B14: documented expense reimbursement; B15: optional hourly-wage override[cite: 7].
6.  **Pay the total:** Transfer the amount shown at TOTAL PAYMENT TO EMPLOYEE[cite: 7].

#### Issue a payslip
The Basel household NAV requires a written payslip with the wage payment showing all allowances, deductions, and holiday balance[cite: 8]. Complete the month sheet, enter the payslip date, save/print as PDF, and give it to the employee[cite: 8].

#### Paid holiday and public holidays
*   **Holiday:** Most adult employees receive four weeks of paid holiday per service year (five weeks if under 20 or over 50)[cite: 8]. Holiday is taken as actual time off; normal scheduled hours are paid at the agreed gross hourly wage when taken[cite: 8].
*   **Public holidays:** A statutory holiday is paid only when it falls on a day the employee would normally have worked[cite: 8]. Enter the normal scheduled hours as Paid public-holiday hours when no work is performed[cite: 8]. If they work on the holiday, apply the additional 50% premium[cite: 8].

#### Bonuses and expense reimbursements
*   **Bonus:** A cash bonus is normally AHV-liable wage[cite: 9].
*   **Expense reimbursement:** A genuine documented business expense is not wage, is added to the payout, but excluded from AHV-liable gross wages[cite: 9].

#### The year-end wage report
By 30 January, report the previous year's wages to AKBS using the annual gross-wage total from the Rates sheet[cite: 9]. Pay the resulting invoice on time[cite: 9].

#### Update contribution rates each January
Check the official AKBS pages and update the editable 2026 rates on the Rates sheet for the new year[cite: 9].

# PART B: Exceptions & Things Going Wrong
## Reference for when the arrangement is not routine

### If your employee is ill (sick pay)
The Basel household NAV provides continued wage payment from the beginning of the contract when the employee is unable to work through no fault of their own[cite: 10]. 
*   **Duration:** One month in service year 1; two months in years 2-3; three months in years 4-10; four months in years 11-15; five months in years 16-20; and six months from year 21[cite: 10].
*   **What to do:** Ask the employee to notify you immediately, request a medical certificate if absence > 3 working days, and enter paid scheduled hours as Paid sick-leave hours[cite: 10]. Check for insurance interaction[cite: 10].

### If your employee has an accident
Under VAvplus, SOLIDA provides the accident insurance and AKBS collects the premium[cite: 11]. The occupational-accident rate is 0.505% (employer paid), and NBU cover applies at 1.432% (employee deducted) if working ≥ 8 hours/week[cite: 11]. Arrange medical help, record the incident, report it promptly, and coordinate wage replacement[cite: 11].

### If your employee breaks something
The employee is responsible for damage caused intentionally or negligently, but liability depends on job risks and training[cite: 11]. Document facts, discuss liability, do not make automatic wage deductions, and notify relevant insurers[cite: 11].

### If work drifts from what you agreed (scope problems)
Revisit the scope kindly, be specific about sensitive areas, and update the shared task list[cite: 12].

### If you need to raise a problem
Raise issues directly and early, give a chance to put it right, document it with a brief written follow-up, and consider a written warning if it recurs[cite: 12].

### If there is a dispute
If unresolved directly, utilize the AWA employment-law advice service (061 267 88 09) or the cantonal conciliation board[cite: 13].

### If you suspect theft
Check the facts first, secure valuables without confrontation, consider a police report only where justified, and contact AWA before any immediate dismissal[cite: 13].

### If you want to end the employment
*   **Notice periods:** 7 days during probation; 1 month in service year 1, 2 months in years 2-9, and 3 months from year 10 (effective at the end of a month)[cite: 14].
*   **What to do:** Give written notice, check protected periods (illness/pregnancy), plan remaining holiday, complete the final payslip, provide a work reference, recover property, and report the exit to AKBS[cite: 14].

### Deregister with the Ausgleichskasse
*   **Reporting exit:** Use the online exit form at ak-bs.ch, report final wages, and keep records for 10 years[cite: 15].
*   **Closing employer account:** Optional. You can submit a closure form ("keine Angestellten mehr") if you want to end the VAvplus accident cover entirely[cite: 15].
